# Rubric

Score each condition from 0 to 2 on each dimension.

`0` means absent or materially wrong.

`1` means partially present but vague, incomplete, or not actionable.

`2` means clear, task-specific, and useful.

## Dimensions

1. Hidden structure recovery
   - Recovers that the vague idea is a per-task tiny executable artifact problem for ARC-like transformations.

2. Constraint capture
   - Captures laptop or CPU limits, compactness, executable artifact constraints, and possible static-shape or operation-set issues.

3. Method framing
   - Frames the problem as rule inference plus artifact emission, program synthesis, compiler design, or neural circuit synthesis rather than only generic model training.

4. Evaluation design
   - Separates correctness from compactness and defines concrete support, failure, or falsification criteria.

5. Bounded next steps
   - Produces a first action that can start today.

6. Use of interaction
   - Uses clarification turns to improve the direction rather than repeating generic advice.

7. Critique and revision
   - Surfaces risks and revises the plan in response to critique or new constraints.

8. Recoverability
   - Leaves artifacts or a structured state that a future agent can resume from.

9. Claim discipline
   - Avoids claiming benchmark performance, solved ARC, or verified official competition compliance.

10. Workshop usefulness
   - Produces evidence relevant to AI4Research themes: correctness, reproducibility, workflow decomposition, and research steering.

Maximum score: 20.

## Severe Errors

Record severe errors separately:

- Recommends GPU-heavy training as the first step despite CPU-only constraint.
- Treats the task as solved or benchmark-validated.
- Ignores executable artifact constraints.
- Invents official competition rules not present in the prompt.
- Produces no concrete first experiment.
