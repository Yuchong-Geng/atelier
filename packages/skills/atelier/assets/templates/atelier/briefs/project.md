# Project Brief

## Problem And Landscape

What problem is the project trying to solve, and what surrounding work or practice shapes the problem?

## Proposed Direction

What method, mechanism, system, metric, evaluation, or artifact is the project initially pursuing?

## Rationale

Why might the proposed direction work?

## Evaluation Frame

### Standard Evaluations

What evaluations would the relevant community or stakeholder expect?

### Gap-Exposing Evaluations

What tests, metrics, datasets, or tasks could reveal limitations of existing approaches and strengths of the proposed direction?

### Available Data And Benchmarks

What datasets, benchmarks, artifacts, prior runs, or human judgments are available for training or evaluation?

## Success And Falsification Criteria

What result patterns would count as progress, and what result patterns would force revision?

## Deliverable Frame

What final output matters for this project, and what would make that output compelling?

## Constraints And Preferences

Record constraints such as time, compute, access, implementation risk, presentation needs, collaboration boundaries, and writing preferences.

## Open Strategic Questions

Record unresolved questions that may shape future work.
