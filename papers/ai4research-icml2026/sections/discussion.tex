\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

Rather than aiming to replace the researcher with an autonomous scientist, Atelier provides a protocol for routing AI-assisted research through layered abstractions and inspectable files. Our pilot evaluations show that rubric scores rise across both sweeps, lifecycle runs produce inspectable artifacts, and the protocol recovers from external failures. Atelier's value lies in the workflow discipline it provides, augmenting researcher judgment rather than replacing it.

In its current form, the pilot is small (eight runs total), manually scored, and tests Atelier on a simulated research scenario rather than a real, full-scale research project. The scorer is a project participant despite a rubric fixed in advance. The paper does not compare Atelier against other agent frameworks or against blind expert judges. Future work includes blind scoring, benchmarking against alternative agent stacks, validation on full-scale research projects with verifiable outcomes, and a thoughtfully designed UI that makes the project artifacts more readable.
